Our Approach

Serving Maryland & the Mid-Atlantic

Legacy Waters Environmental Services provides comprehensive solutions to restore and maintain healthy ponds, lakes, and waterways across Maryland and the Mid-Atlantic region. From small community ponds to large commercial lakes and stormwater systems, we focus on long-term performance, safety, and aesthetics. Every project is approached with careful assessment and a commitment to protecting the communities and properties that depend on these water assets.

Comprehensive Pond Cleaning and Restoration

Comprehensive pond cleaning is at the core of what we do. Rather than treating problems in isolation, we look at sediment, shoreline stability, vegetation, debris, and inflow/outflow performance as a complete system. Our pond cleaning services often combine dredging, vegetation management, and debris removal to return ponds to a healthy, functional state. The result is a cleaner, safer, and more resilient waterway that is easier to maintain and enjoy for years to come.

Hydraulic Dredging Services

We specialize in hydraulic dredging to safely remove accumulated sediment and restore proper depth in ponds, lakes, and canals. By carefully evaluating each site before mobilizing, we minimize disruption while maximizing dredging efficiency. Restoring depth and capacity helps improve water quality, reduce flooding risk, and extend the life of existing infrastructure.

Aquatic Vegetation, Trash, and Debris Removal

Overgrown or invasive vegetation can quickly turn a healthy waterway into a clogged and unattractive environment. Our aquatic vegetation removal services target nuisance species while respecting the broader ecosystem, reopening waterways for recreation and improving overall habitat balance. We also remove floating and submerged trash and debris that create hazards and visual blight, helping owners maintain clean, navigable, and professional-looking waterways.
